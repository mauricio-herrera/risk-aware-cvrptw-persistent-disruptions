# External Athens last-mile delivery data

The real operational instances used in this study originate from the published
Athens last-mile delivery dataset:

Vrani, I., Apostolidis, P., Kapoutsis, A., & Kosmatopoulos, E. (2025).
Real-world last-mile delivery data for pharmaceutical distribution in Athens.
Data in Brief, 61, 111762.

DOI:
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2025.111762

The original source dataset is not redistributed in this repository.

Compact derived tables and frozen confirmatory computational outputs required
to reproduce the results reported in the manuscript are included where
appropriate.

The Hawkes disruption process, experimental vehicle capacities, and controlled
service-window stress settings used in the study should not be interpreted as
empirical estimates from historical Athens disruption data.

The Hawkes process is used as a controlled exogenous model of temporally
persistent disruption.
