## Athens operational dataset

The underlying real operational instances originate from the published Athens
last-mile delivery dataset:

Vrani, I., Apostolidis, P., Kapoutsis, A., & Kosmatopoulos, E. (2025).
Data in Brief, 61, 111762.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2025.111762

The original source dataset is not redistributed in this archive.
